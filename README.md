# 🛒 ShopScope SQL Analytics

## E-commerce Sales Analysis

ShopScope SQL Analytics is a compact data analytics project that uses
SQL to analyze product sales, revenue, and sales volume for an
e-commerce business.

## 🎯 Business Questions

This project answers:

-   How many orders were recorded?
-   How much total revenue was generated?
-   Which product generated the most revenue?
-   Which product sold the most units?
-   Which category generated the most revenue?
-   What was the average order value?

## 🔎 Key Insights

-   The dataset contains **20 orders**.
-   Total revenue reached **\$10,960**.
-   **Laptop** generated the highest product revenue at **\$6,000**.
-   **Mouse** recorded the highest sales volume with **16 units sold**.
-   **Computers** was the highest-revenue category at **\$6,000**.
-   Average order value was **\$548**.

## 💡 Business Recommendation

Revenue and sales volume should be evaluated together when assessing
product performance. High-revenue products such as Laptop contribute
strongly to total sales value, while high-volume products such as Mouse
may create opportunities for bundles, cross-selling, and promotions.
